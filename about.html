About Stephenie Tocado · Owner

Two decades of mortgage. One standard of care.

Star Processing was founded by Stephenie Tocado to give loan officers and brokers a reliable, detail-driven partner — built on real estate, title, and processing experience accumulated since 2002.

With over 20 years of experience in the mortgage and real estate industry, I bring a comprehensive understanding of the loan process from application to closing.

Since 2002, I have worked across mortgage, real estate, and title — giving me a unique ability to manage every aspect of the transaction efficiently and effectively. I understand how each piece of the process connects, and how to keep everything moving toward a successful closing.

I have experience with all major loan types, including conventional, FHA, VA, jumbo, non-QM, condo, and equity lines, allowing me to step into any file with confidence and precision. I work routinely with leading lenders including Rocket Mortgage, Click N Close, and Spring EQ, among many others.

I am a Licensed Loan Officer in North Carolina and a Licensed Mortgage Broker in South Carolina (NMLS #2636616) — credentials that ensure every file meets state and federal regulatory standards.

In addition to loan processing, I assist mortgage professionals with compliance-related tasks, including quarterly and annual call reports and maintaining organization for NMLS and state regulatory requirements.

I founded Star Processing to provide a reliable, detail-driven third-party processing solution for loan officers and brokers who want to grow their business without sacrificing service, efficiency, or compliance.

My approach is proactive, communicative, and focused on delivering smooth, on-time closings while protecting your client relationships and reputation.
