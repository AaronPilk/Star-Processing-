Services

A full toolkit for clean, on-time closings.

From application to clear-to-close, Star Processing manages every milestone. We handle the moving pieces so loan officers and brokers can focus on what they do best — originating.

01

Full-Service Mortgage Processing

Complete file management from application through clear-to-close. We manage communication, documentation, and coordination across every stakeholder to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

02

All Loan Types

We work with a wide range of loan products and step into any file with confidence and precision.

  • Conventional
  • FHA
  • VA
  • USDA
  • Jumbo
  • Non-QM & DSCR
  • Investment Property Loans
  • HELOCs & Home Equity Lines
03

Pre-Underwriting File Structuring

We review income, assets, and credit upfront to identify potential issues before submission — helping ensure clean, complete files and removing surprises down the line.

04

Condition Management & File Acceleration

We aggressively manage and clear underwriting conditions, keeping files moving and preventing unnecessary delays. Proactive follow-through, not waiting for the next email.

05

Realtor & Title Coordination

We communicate directly with agents and title companies to keep alignment, protect relationships, and maintain the closing timeline. Your borrowers and your referral partners feel the difference.

06

Pipeline & Communication Management

Consistent updates, milestone tracking, and proactive communication so you always know exactly where your files stand. No black-box processing.

07

Compliance & Call Report Support

We assist with the back-office discipline that keeps your business healthy and audit-ready — well beyond the loan file.

  • Quarterly & annual call reports
  • Nationwide Multistate Licensing System (NMLS) requirements
  • State regulatory reporting
  • Documentation organization
